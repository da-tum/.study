Computational Linguistics

Panini: The World's First Formal Language Specification

Panini's Ashtadhyayi composed ~4,000 algorithmic rules using auxiliary symbols (it-markers) and meta-rules (paribhāṣā). It generates the entire Sanskrit phonology and morphology through context-sensitive derivation steps.

⚡ Modern Computer Science Parallel

Direct historical precursor to Backus-Naur Form (BNF) used in compiler construction, context-free grammars (Noam Chomsky), and modern production rule expert systems.
