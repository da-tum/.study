Theoretical Syllabus & Units

Unit 01

Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrences

Mathematical definitions of O, Ω, Θ, o, ω. Master Theorem derivation, substitution induction proofs, and recursion tree geometric summation.

Master Theorem Substitution Method Recursion Tree Growth Rankings
Ready for Notes
Unit 02

Divide and Conquer Strategies

Recursive problem partitioning, combine step complexities, QuickSort pivot strategies, Strassen's matrix algorithm, and Closest Pair of Points in 2D plane.

MergeSort Inversions QuickSort Randomized Strassen Matrix Closest Pair O(n log n)
Ready for Notes
Unit 03

Greedy Paradigms & Matroids

Locally optimal choices that guarantee global optimality. Greedy-choice property, optimal substructure, Huffman prefix-free encoding, and Minimum Spanning Trees.

Fractional Knapsack Huffman Coding Tree Kruskal's & Prim's MST Activity Selection
Ready for Notes
Unit 04

Dynamic Programming Framework

Overlapping subproblems and memoization tables. Bellman equations, state transition matrices, sequence alignments, and all-pairs shortest paths.

0/1 Knapsack DP Longest Common Subsequence Matrix Chain Multiplication Floyd-Warshall
Ready for Notes
Unit 05

Backtracking & Branch and Bound

Systematic state-space tree traversals with bounding functions. Pruning non-promising paths for NP-hard combinatorial optimizations.

N-Queens Problem Graph Coloring (m-colors) Hamiltonian Cycles TSP Branch & Bound
Ready for Notes
Unit 06

Complexity Classes (P, NP, NP-C)

Deterministic vs Nondeterministic Polynomial Time. Polynomial-time verification, reductions (A ≤p B), Cook-Levin theorem, and 3-SAT reduction chains.

P vs NP Concepts NP-Complete Proofs Vertex Cover & Clique Approximation Ratios
Ready for Notes
